Logo refinements: three marks, five ways each, and Diff on a clipboard

Diff, Tower and Horizon from the first sheet, each taken five ways: the original tightened, an alternate construction, a badge for tiles and catalog cards, a stroke or one-colour version, and a lockup. Diff gets three more on a clipboard. Every card pairs its mark with one of the three wordmark treatments. The kept cards go further on the detail sheet: hairline rules, more detail, flags. No gradients, glows or bevels anywhere: flat ink, one accent, and the deep green only on the badges.

How to read a card. The large mark is for the masthead and reports; on the lockup cards the whole lockup is the large view. Under it, the mark beside its wordmark, then the favicon at 32 and 16 pixels, the 16-pixel icon rasterised once at 1× and blown up four times, an app tile and a browser tab. Badges keep their deep green in both themes; everything else follows the site's ink and accent.

What changed from the first sheet

  • Diff: signs and lines now differ in cap and weight, and the old line is shorter and greyer than the new one, so the pair reads as a change rather than a list.
  • Tower: the walls taper and the parapet overhangs, with a slit or door cut through, so it is a building and not a crenellated box.
  • Horizon: the sun is clipped by the line instead of balanced on it, or drawn as an arc, so it stops looking like a mound.

Badge colour

  • The badge green is #163229, the accent darkened until the mint accent and pale knockout read on it; it does not change with the theme.
  • On the badges the red and green are the dark-theme tints, which are the ones that hold up on a dark ground.

Notes

  • The lockup cards set the name in the page's Newsreader; a final lockup would be outlined to paths so it needs no font.
  • Mockup page uses inline styles, a little script for the theme switch, the 1× loupe and the lockup rule length, and Google Fonts; it is not served by the app.
