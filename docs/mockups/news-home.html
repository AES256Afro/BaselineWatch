BleepingComputer's shape on BaselineWatch's tokens: areas across the top, the working sidebar on the right, a featured row of image cards, and the latest list with thumbnails. News is home at /. Every image is pulled passively at ingest from data the aggregates already deliver, cached in the content-addressed blob store, and served from /img/<hash> — required anyway, because the app's own CSP says img-src 'self', which forbids hot-linking.

Top bar and sidebar

Areas across the top (five words, never wraps); the right rail carries the working boxes — KEV, register changes, velocity — and becomes the owner's queue when signed in. Exactly BleepingComputer's division: nav up top, “Popular” rail beside the list.

The register stays present

The amber strip, the sidebar box, and REG items inside the feed: the register is woven through the news page rather than being a sibling tab only.

Mobile

The rail drops below the list on narrow screens; featured row becomes a swipe strip of two-up cards. Thumbnails hide under 480px, keeping the phone list text-first like today's Wire.

Getting the images — passively

  1. Feed media, free with the fetch.

    Most aggregated feeds already carry the lead image: RSS <enclosure type="image/…"> or <media:content>/<media:thumbnail> (The Hacker News, The Record), or an <img> inside content:encoded, which feed.ts already reads for summaries. Zero extra requests: parse it out as imageUrl on ParsedItem.

  2. og:image fallback, one small request per new article.

    For feeds with no media (BleepingComputer's own RSS is text-only), fetch the article page once at ingest — same polite HTTP client, rate limits and snapshot discipline as everything else — and read <meta property="og:image">. One GET per new item, ever; never on page view. A per-source flag turns it off.

  3. Cache into the blob store, serve from self.

    Download the image once, downscale to ~640px WebP, store content-addressed in blobs.ts, keep the hash on the item, serve at /img/<hash>. The CSP stays img-src 'self', readers' IPs never touch third-party servers, and dead images can't rot the page.

  4. No image → typographic placeholder.

    NVD, CISA directives and standards releases have no art; they get the section-mark placeholder (fourth card above) — a deliberate look, not a broken one.

Licensing note

Small thumbnails beside headlines that link out is standard aggregator practice, and feed-supplied media is published for exactly this use. Keep them small (≤640px), always linked to the source, with the og:image fetch off by default for any source whose terms object — same spirit as the CIS rule in AGENTS.md.

Schema

One migration: items.image_blob (nullable hash) + image_origin (enclosure · body · og). Adapters stay one module each; the og fetch lives in the ingester, not per-adapter.

What not to do

No hot-linking (CSP forbids it, and it leaks reader IPs), no fetching at render time, no re-crawling old items — images are picked up only as new items arrive, which is what “passive” should mean.
