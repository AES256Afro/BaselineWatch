Today — five directions

The page as it stands is a dashboard: fifteen blocks, eight tiles and two charts that mostly read zero. These five each pick one job for /today and drop everything that does not serve it. All five obey the same two rules — no decorative chart (a figure appears only with the comparison that gives it meaning, written out), and the published fix sits beside the thing it fixes.

Mock-up, invented data. Source names, standards and fleet releases are the real ones (src/adapters/, data/platforms.yaml); the CVE numbers, KB numbers and counts are made up to show the shape. All five are drawn on the app's real Register-look tokens on purpose, so the comparison is about what the page says and in what order, not about colour. Owner's view throughout.

Thesis

Thesis

Teach the ladder by drawing it every morning. The change register — the one thing no other tool does — becomes the page's spine instead of a sidebar.

Strong at

The public teaching site. A reader with no fleet still learns something, and the "and the vulnerabilities, against the controls they test" band is a genuinely new way to read a CVE.

Costs

Fits
