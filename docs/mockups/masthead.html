Reorganising the top — ten directions, news first

Three decisions run through all ten: News is the default landing (the Wire renamed and served at /, the way every leading aggregator lands you on the feed); the eleven areas collapse into a few top-level groups; and the phrase Standards change register leaves the wordmark — today it sits as a shouting-caps appendage on every page — for a place that earns it, different in each direction and flagged in amber below. cybercard.net and the BoxPilot install on BigBox run the same image, so each direction is one layout.ts; the sites differ only by sign-in.

Precedent

What the leading aggregators agree on

News is the front door

The Hacker News, BleepingComputer, SANS ISC, DarkReading, SecurityWeek — every one lands on the feed. Dashboards (Feedly TI, Recorded Future) land on today's intelligence. Nobody lands in the reference library.

Five-ish top entries

CISA folded hundreds of pages into ~6 headings; THN runs seven short words. Groups, not a tab per corpus.

Reference behind one door

NVD is search-first with the tables one level down; ISC keeps “Data” as one entry. The Register/STIGs/Baselines/Atlas quartet is one Standards door.

The desk is set apart

Feedly separates your boards from the feeds; Recorded Future badges your queue. Triage, Watchlist, Digest, Sources read as one owner cluster, absent until signed in.

Recommendation

Direction 2's shape, Direction 6's sentence

Top: News (home, at /) · Today · Standards ▾ (Register, STIGs, Baselines, Atlas) · Vulnerabilities, with the owner's Desk (Triage · Watchlist · Digest · Sources, triage count as badge) folded to the right, rendering as “Owner sign-in” on cybercard.net. Five words, one dropdown, one <details> — no script, CSP untouched.

The register's phrase: take it off the wordmark. On the home page it reads as Direction 6's motto — “A change register for the security standards themselves” — and the Register strip from Direction 2 carries the three latest standards changes under the masthead on News and Today, so the differentiator is shown as working headlines, not asserted as a label. Inside the Standards menu it captions the group (Direction 4).

Why not the rest: D5 and D8 are the right ideas for a future Console-look refinement (badges, rail) but fork the layout; D10 taxes every visit; D9 is the safest no-dropdown fallback if the <details> menu feels wrong in testing.

Routes: / → News (Wire renamed), /register → Register, old /wire 301s home. One change in src/pages/layout.ts plus route aliases aligns cybercard.net and BigBox at the next image pull.
