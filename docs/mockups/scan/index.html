Scan ten mockups

A Scan area for BaselineWatch: the browser-side ad and DNS signals taken from a public scan page's technique, and three eyes that page cannot have because this server sits on the LAN: the network's resolver, the router, and the firewall from both sides. Plus device scripts, a manual guide, the "why" with its sources, the Console look, and history with alerts. Sample data throughout; the look is the app's own (the Register tokens from public/app.css), none of the other site's interface. The milestones are M12 in docs/PLAN.md.

Every page carries breadcrumbs and a "Why it matters" line per check that links into mockup 08, which is where the explanatory prose lives, rewritten and sourced. The browser check is the one page that would carry a script; the plan says how its policy is scoped.
