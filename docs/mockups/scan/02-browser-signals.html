Limits of this check

  • Signals, not a safety certificate. Element hiding and failed requests cannot prove ad code was stopped.
  • It sees this browser only; the resolver rows report this browser's resolver, not the device's.
  • Effective filters can be missed: Pi-hole, a VPN's filter and most browser filters have no self-check. The network check is the better eye for those.
  • Blocking ads reduces exposure; ads served from the content's own domain are not blocked by any of this.

Privacy of the test

Test images carry no cookies and no referrer and are never shown. The only outside hosts contacted are the two resolvers' self-check endpoints, each with a fresh random name. No advertising domain is contacted; the canaries in the network check are resolved by this server, not by your browser.
