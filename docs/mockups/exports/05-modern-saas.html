Decisions 10 of 212

BW-101Limit consecutive failed logon attempts
Modified
Publisher recommends
Lock the account after 3 consecutive invalid attempts within 15 minutes.
APPL-26-000022 · CAT IIpwpolicy_account_lockout_enforceAC-7
We decided
Lock after 5 attempts; 15-minute lockout.
Sign-in uses Platform SSO with Entra ID, which has its own lockout. At 3 attempts the pilot had 40+ helpdesk unlocks a week.
