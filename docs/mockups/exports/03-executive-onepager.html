Appendix A — Decision register

10 of 212 decisions shown.

BW-101 · Limit consecutive failed logon attemptsModified
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-000022 CAT II · pwpolicy_account_lockout_enforce · AC-7
Lock the account after 3 consecutive invalid attempts within 15 minutes.
Our decision · Endpoint Engineering
Lock after 5 attempts; 15-minute lockout.
Sign-in uses Platform SSO with Entra ID, which has its own lockout. At 3 attempts the pilot had 40+ helpdesk unlocks a week.
BW-102 · Enforce smart card authenticationNot applying
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-000060 CAT II · auth_smartcard_enforce · IA-2(1), IA-2(2), IA-2(12)
Require a PIV/CAC smart card for login and authorisation.
Our decision · Identity and Access
Platform SSO with a Secure Enclave key (phishing-resistant).
PIV cards are not issued to macOS users. MFA is met by the Platform SSO Secure Enclave key with Entra ID Conditional Access. Risk accepted: EXC-2026-007.
BW-103 · Enforce FileVault and escrow the recovery keyApply
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-005020 CAT II · system_settings_filevault_enforce · SC-28, SC-28(1)
Enable FileVault on all volumes; escrow the recovery key to the MDM.
Our decision · Endpoint Engineering
As recommended; personal recovery key escrowed to Intune or Jamf Pro.
No deviation.
BW-104 · Enable the application firewallApply
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-005050 CAT II · system_settings_firewall_enable · SC-7, SC-7(12)
Turn on the macOS application firewall.
Our decision · Endpoint Engineering
As recommended, plus stealth mode.
No deviation.
BW-105 · Disable SSH (Remote Login)Modified
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-000100 CAT II · system_settings_ssh_disable · CM-7, AC-17
Disable the SSH server unless documented as an operational requirement.
Our decision · Platform Engineering
Off on all endpoints except 14 build hosts, where only the admin group may connect.
CI agents on the iOS build hosts need SSH. Limited to one Smart Group, key auth only, FIPS ciphers (BW-106), logged to the SIEM.
BW-106 · Use FIPS-validated SSH ciphers and MACsApply
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-000110 CAT I · os_sshd_fips_compliant · AC-17(2), IA-7, SC-13
Limit sshd to FIPS 140-validated ciphers, MACs and key exchange.
Our decision · Platform Engineering
As recommended on the 14 hosts where SSH is enabled.
SSH is on for build hosts (BW-105).
BW-107 · Configure audit flags and retentionApply
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-001010 CAT II · audit_flags_lo_configure · AU-2, AU-12, AU-11
Audit logon/logoff, admin and authorisation events; retain locally per policy.
Our decision · Security Operations
As recommended; 60-day local retention, forwarded to the SIEM.
No deviation.
BW-108 · Enforce software update installationApply
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-002050 CAT II · system_settings_software_update_download_enforce · SI-2, SI-2(2)
Download and install security updates automatically.
Our decision · Endpoint Engineering
Declarative device management enforces minor updates within 7 days.
No deviation.
BW-109 · Lock the screen after 15 minutes of inactivityApply
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-000070 CAT II · system_settings_screensaver_timeout_enforce · AC-11, AC-11(1)
Start the screen saver after at most 15 minutes and require a password immediately.
Our decision · Endpoint Engineering
As recommended (900 seconds, password on wake).
No deviation.
BW-110 · Disable BluetoothNot applying
Recommended · STIG APPL-26-002080 CAT II · system_settings_bluetooth_disable · AC-18, AC-18(3)
Turn off Bluetooth unless an approved peripheral requires it.
Our decision · Endpoint Engineering
Bluetooth on; sharing and discoverability off.
Keyboards, headsets and Continuity need Bluetooth. Bluetooth Sharing stays off. Risk accepted: EXC-2026-008.
