1.0 Executive summary

The assessment examined, interviewed and tested the macOS 26 “Tahoe” baseline against 212 rules drawn from the DISA STIG and the NIST mSCP High baseline, mapped to SP 800-53 Rev. 5 controls. 186 rules are applied as published, 14 are applied with an organisation-defined value and 12 are not applied under a documented risk acceptance. Of 24 test procedures, 19 were satisfied, 3 were other than satisfied, 1 was not applicable and 1 remains to be run.

2.0 Baseline decisions

Each entry states the publisher's recommendation and the organisation's decision. Entries shown are those requiring an authorising official's attention plus a representative sample of applied rules (10 of 212).

BW-101 · Limit consecutive failed logon attemptsAC-7
Publisher recommendationLock the account after 3 consecutive invalid attempts within 15 minutes.
STIG APPL-26-000022 (CAT II) · mSCP pwpolicy_account_lockout_enforce
Organisation decisionModified. Lock after 5 attempts; 15-minute lockout.
Justification: Users sign in through Platform SSO with Entra ID, whose smart lockout also applies. A threshold of 3 drove 40+ helpdesk unlocks a week during the pilot; 5 keeps AC-7 intent with the cloud lockout as a second layer. Owner: Endpoint Engineering. Verified by: T-AUTH-06.
BW-102 · Enforce smart card authenticationIA-2(1), IA-2(2), IA-2(12)
Publisher recommendationRequire a PIV/CAC smart card for login and authorisation.
STIG APPL-26-000060 (CAT II) · mSCP auth_smartcard_enforce
Organisation decisionNot applying. Platform SSO with a Secure Enclave key (phishing-resistant).
Justification: PIV cards are not issued to macOS users. Multifactor, phishing-resistant authentication is met by the Platform SSO Secure Enclave key bound to Entra ID with Conditional Access. Risk accepted as EXC-2026-007. Owner: Identity and Access. Verified by: T-AUTH-07.
BW-103 · Enforce FileVault and escrow the recovery keySC-28, SC-28(1)
Publisher recommendationEnable FileVault on all volumes; escrow the recovery key to the MDM.
STIG APPL-26-005020 (CAT II) · mSCP system_settings_filevault_enforce
Organisation decisionApply. As recommended; personal recovery key escrowed to Intune or Jamf Pro.
Justification: No deviation. Owner: Endpoint Engineering. Verified by: T-DISK-01.
BW-104 · Enable the application firewallSC-7, SC-7(12)
Publisher recommendationTurn on the macOS application firewall.
STIG APPL-26-005050 (CAT II) · mSCP system_settings_firewall_enable
Organisation decisionApply. As recommended, plus stealth mode.
Justification: No deviation. Owner: Endpoint Engineering. Verified by: T-NET-01.
BW-105 · Disable SSH (Remote Login)CM-7, AC-17
Publisher recommendationDisable the SSH server unless documented as an operational requirement.
STIG APPL-26-000100 (CAT II) · mSCP system_settings_ssh_disable
Organisation decisionModified. Off on all endpoints except 14 build hosts, where only the admin group may connect.
Justification: The iOS build farm needs SSH for CI agents. Limited to a named Smart Group, keys only, FIPS ciphers (BW-106) and logged to the SIEM. Owner: Platform Engineering. Verified by: T-NET-04.
BW-106 · Use FIPS-validated SSH ciphers and MACsAC-17(2), IA-7, SC-13
Publisher recommendationLimit sshd to FIPS 140-validated ciphers, MACs and key exchange.
STIG APPL-26-000110 (CAT I) · mSCP os_sshd_fips_compliant
Organisation decisionApply. As recommended on the 14 hosts where SSH is enabled.
Justification: Tested only because BW-105 leaves SSH on for build hosts. Owner: Platform Engineering. Verified by: T-NET-03.
BW-107 · Configure audit flags and retentionAU-2, AU-12, AU-11
Publisher recommendationAudit logon/logoff, admin and authorisation events; retain locally per policy.
STIG APPL-26-001010 (CAT II) · mSCP audit_flags_lo_configure
Organisation decisionApply. As recommended; 60-day local retention, forwarded to the SIEM.
Justification: No deviation. Owner: Security Operations. Verified by: T-LOG-01.
BW-108 · Enforce software update installationSI-2, SI-2(2)
Publisher recommendationDownload and install security updates automatically.
STIG APPL-26-002050 (CAT II) · mSCP system_settings_software_update_download_enforce
Organisation decisionApply. Declarative device management enforces minor updates within 7 days.
Justification: No deviation. Owner: Endpoint Engineering. Verified by: T-UPD-01.
BW-109 · Lock the screen after 15 minutes of inactivityAC-11, AC-11(1)
Publisher recommendationStart the screen saver after at most 15 minutes and require a password immediately.
STIG APPL-26-000070 (CAT II) · mSCP system_settings_screensaver_timeout_enforce
Organisation decisionApply. As recommended (900 seconds, password on wake).
Justification: No deviation. Owner: Endpoint Engineering. Verified by: T-AUTH-05.
BW-110 · Disable BluetoothAC-18, AC-18(3)
Publisher recommendationTurn off Bluetooth unless an approved peripheral requires it.
STIG APPL-26-002080 (CAT II) · mSCP system_settings_bluetooth_disable
Organisation decisionNot applying. Bluetooth on; sharing and discoverability off.
Justification: Keyboards, headsets and Continuity are business-essential. Bluetooth Sharing stays disabled (applied control). Risk accepted as EXC-2026-008. Owner: Endpoint Engineering.
