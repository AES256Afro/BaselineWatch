Decision register (10/212)

BW-101 Limit consecutive failed logon attempts[MODIFIED]
// publisher
Lock the account after 3 consecutive invalid attempts within 15 minutes.
APPL-26-000022 cat=II
pwpolicy_account_lockout_enforce
AC-7
// ours · owner=Endpoint Engineering
Lock after 5 attempts; 15-minute lockout.
Users sign in through Platform SSO with Entra ID, whose smart lockout also applies. A threshold of 3 drove 40+ helpdesk unlocks a week during the pilot; 5 keeps AC-7 intent with the cloud lockout as a second layer.
BW-102 Enforce smart card authentication[NOT APPLYING]
// publisher
Require a PIV/CAC smart card for login and authorisation.
APPL-26-000060 cat=II
auth_smartcard_enforce
IA-2(1) IA-2(2) IA-2(12)
// ours · owner=Identity and Access
Platform SSO with a Secure Enclave key (phishing-resistant).
PIV cards are not issued to macOS users. Multifactor, phishing-resistant authentication is met by the Platform SSO Secure Enclave key bound to Entra ID with Conditional Access. Risk accepted as EXC-2026-007.
