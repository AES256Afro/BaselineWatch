1 Executive summary

2 Decision register

Each card sets the publisher's recommendation beside our decision. Shown: 10 of 212.

BW-101 · Limit consecutive failed logon attempts — Modified
Publisher recommendationLock the account after 3 consecutive invalid attempts within 15 minutes.Our decision: ModifiedLock after 5 attempts; 15-minute lockout.
ReferencesSTIG APPL-26-000022 (CAT II)
mSCP pwpolicy_account_lockout_enforce
SP 800-53: AC-7		Justification · ownerUsers sign in through Platform SSO with Entra ID, whose smart lockout also applies. A threshold of 3 drove 40+ helpdesk unlocks a week during the pilot; 5 keeps AC-7 intent with the cloud lockout as a second layer.
Endpoint Engineering · test T-AUTH-06
BW-102 · Enforce smart card authentication — Not applying
Publisher recommendationRequire a PIV/CAC smart card for login and authorisation.Our decision: Not applyingPlatform SSO with a Secure Enclave key (phishing-resistant).
ReferencesSTIG APPL-26-000060 (CAT II)
mSCP auth_smartcard_enforce
SP 800-53: IA-2(1), IA-2(2), IA-2(12)		Justification · ownerPIV cards are not issued to macOS users. Multifactor, phishing-resistant authentication is met by the Platform SSO Secure Enclave key bound to Entra ID with Conditional Access. Risk accepted as EXC-2026-007.
Identity and Access · test T-AUTH-07
BW-103 · Enforce FileVault and escrow the recovery key — Apply
Publisher recommendationEnable FileVault on all volumes; escrow the recovery key to the MDM.Our decision: ApplyAs recommended; personal recovery key escrowed to Intune or Jamf Pro.
ReferencesSTIG APPL-26-005020 (CAT II)
mSCP system_settings_filevault_enforce
SP 800-53: SC-28, SC-28(1)		Justification · ownerNo deviation.
Endpoint Engineering · test T-DISK-01
